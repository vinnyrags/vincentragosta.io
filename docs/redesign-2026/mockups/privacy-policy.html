01 / Overview

Your privacy matters.

This site is a personal portfolio operated by Vincent Ragosta. It is not an e-commerce platform, does not sell products or services online, and does not run advertising. The information below describes every way this site interacts with your data.

02 / Information collected

Only what you send.

Contact form submissions

This site includes a contact form powered by WPForms. When you submit the form, the information you provide — your name, email address, and message — is delivered via email. Form submissions are not stored in a database and are not shared with third parties.

No analytics or tracking

This site does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or tracking service. No behavioral data is collected and no visitor activity is monitored or recorded.

03 / Third-party services

Nothing phones home.

Self-hosted fonts

This site's typefaces are served directly from this site. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service when you visit a page.

Yoast SEO

Yoast generates metadata, XML sitemaps, and structured data for search engines. It operates entirely on the server and does not collect or transmit visitor data to third parties.

04 / Cookies

Minimal cookie usage.

No tracking or advertising cookies. WordPress may set a small number of functional cookies related to site operation — for example, to keep you logged in if you have an account.

05 / Your rights

Access, correction, deletion.

If you have submitted information through the contact form and would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of that data, reach me at hello@vincentragosta.io.

06 / Changes

When this changes.

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the site's functionality or legal requirements. Updates are posted on this page with a revised date.
