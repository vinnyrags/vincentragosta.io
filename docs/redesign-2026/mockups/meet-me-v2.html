01 / Working philosophy

What I optimize for.

I believe the best digital work comes from understanding the full picture — not just the design or the code, but the business problem behind both. Every project I take on starts with listening: what's the goal, who's it for, and what does success look like?

I write code that's meant to last. That means strict types, automated testing, semantic markup, and accessibility built in from the start — not bolted on at the end. I care about performance, maintainability, and giving the next developer (or future me) something clear to work with.

01Full-stack ownership

I move comfortably between frontend and backend, from React editor components to PHP architecture to CI pipelines.

02Convention over configuration

Predictable patterns reduce onboarding friction and cognitive overhead. If the structure is obvious, the code explains itself.

03Ship, measure, iterate

I'd rather ship something focused and refine it than chase perfection in isolation.

02 / AI integration

How AI fits into
my workflow.

Use AI to move faster on the work that benefits from speed — and stay hands-on for the decisions that benefit from experience.

Where AI accelerates

The work that benefits from speed.

  • Research & exploration — surveying approaches and prototyping before committing to a direction.
  • First drafts — boilerplate and implementations, pair-programmed through the edge cases.
  • Tests & documentation — scaffolds and docs that keep a growing codebase consistent.
  • A second pair of eyes — catching blind spots in code review.
Where I stay hands-on

The decisions that benefit from experience.

  • Architecture — I don't hand over the decisions the rest of the system depends on.
  • Judgment — knowing what good output looks like, and when to override it.
  • Verification — every AI-assisted change is reviewed, tested, and understood.
  • What ships — the context and the taste are still the human's job.
I don't ship code I don't understand.runbooks · dry runs · verified releases
